WELCOME ADDRESS BY THE RECTOR OF THE GATEWAY (ICT) POLYTECHNIC SAAPADE OGUN STATE, Dr SANNI KEHINDE OSENI, AT THE INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (ICONFST’26), HELD FROM 23RD TO 26TH AUGUST, 2026

Protocol

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you all to this International Conference on Science and Technology 2026 (ICONFST’26), organized by the School of Science and Technology of The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade,

Today marks another significant milestone in the history of our great polytechnic as we gather scholars, researchers, innovators, industrialists, policymakers, entrepreneurs, students, and development partners to deliberate on issues that are critical to the advancement of science, technology, innovation, and sustainable national development.

I am delighted to welcome our highly esteemed keynote speaker, Engr. Dr. Rilwan Olaolu Oliyide, our distinguished lead paper presenter, Prof. Sojinu Olatunbosun Samuel, principal officers of the Polytechnic, representatives of tertiary institutions, captains of industry, members of professional bodies, members of staff, students, members of the press, guests, ladies and gentlemen.

Your presence at this conference is a testimony to our shared commitment to advancing knowledge and finding innovative solutions to the numerous developmental challenges confronting our nation and the global community.

The theme of this year’s conference, “Sustainable Research from Gown to Town: Bridging the Academia and Industry Divide,” is both timely and thought-provoking. Across the world, polytechnics are increasingly expected to produce research that extends beyond academic publications to practical innovations capable of solving real-life problems.

For too long, valuable research findings have remained on library shelves and in conference proceedings without translating into products, services, technologies, or policies that positively impact society. This conference therefore seeks to bridge that gap by promoting stronger collaboration between academic institutions and industry.

Research should not merely satisfy academic curiosity; it should improve agricultural productivity, strengthen healthcare delivery, promote environmental sustainability, enhance food security, create employment opportunities, improve manufacturing processes, advance digital technology, and ultimately improve the quality of life of our people.

As a Polytechnic, our mandate has always been rooted in practical knowledge, technological advancement, innovation, entrepreneurship, and skills development. Therefore, this conference aligns perfectly with our vision of producing graduates who are not only academically sound but also equipped with the competencies required to drive industrial growth and national development.

The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade has continued to position itself as a centre of excellence in technology-driven education. Over the years, we have strengthened our academic programmes, upgraded our laboratories, expanded research opportunities, embraced digital transformation, and encouraged innovation among staff and students.

Hosting this international conference is another demonstration of our unwavering commitment to becoming a leading institution where ideas are transformed into practical solutions for societal development.

Distinguished participants, no nation can attain meaningful development without investing in research and innovation. Countries that have become global leaders did so because they deliberately connected research institutions with industries, encouraged commercialization of research findings, and invested in technology-driven solutions.

Nigeria possesses enormous intellectual capacity. What is required is stronger collaboration among researchers, industries, government agencies, investors, and development partners. Conferences like this provide the platform to initiate those collaborations and develop practical pathways for transforming ideas into tangible outcomes.

I therefore encourage all participants to take full advantage of the opportunities this conference provides. Beyond presenting papers, let us engage in meaningful discussions, exchange ideas, establish research networks, develop partnerships, and explore opportunities for collaborative projects that will benefit our institutions and society at large.

To our students, this conference presents a unique learning opportunity. You have the privilege of interacting with renowned scholars and professionals whose experiences can inspire your academic journey and future careers. I encourage you to participate actively, ask questions, and embrace innovation as a tool for solving societal problems.

I wish to express my profound appreciation to the Dean of the School of Science and Technology, Mr Israel Olusegun Eleyowo, for his visionary leadership and dedication in making this conference a reality. My sincere appreciation also goes to the Chairman of the Local Organising Committee, Mr Olujimi Oni, members of the organising committee, sponsors, partners, volunteers, and everyone who contributed their time, expertise, and resources towards the successful planning of this conference.

I also appreciate our keynote speaker, lead paper presenter, session chairpersons, reviewers, and every researcher who responded to our call for papers. Your contributions will undoubtedly enrich the quality of discourse throughout this conference.

As we begin this intellectual engagement, I urge us all to approach every session with open minds, mutual respect, and a willingness to learn from one another. Let our deliberations be driven not only by the pursuit of knowledge but also by the desire to create lasting solutions that will benefit our communities, industries, and our nation.

It is my sincere hope that at the end of this conference, new partnerships will have been established, innovative ideas generated, practical recommendations developed, and sustainable solutions proposed for addressing the numerous challenges confronting our society.

May the outcomes of this conference contribute significantly to scientific advancement, industrial development, policy formulation, economic growth, environmental sustainability, and improved human well-being.

I wish you fruitful deliberations, rewarding interactions, a pleasant stay in Saapade, and safe journeys back to your respective destinations.

With great pleasure, I, Dr Sanni Kehinde Oseni hereby declare this International Conference on Science and Technology (ICONFST’26) officially open.

Thank you, and God bless you all.
